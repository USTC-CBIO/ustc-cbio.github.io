What we study

The CBIO Lab develops AI methods for spatial omics. We enhance spatial gene expression, reconstruct continuous three-dimensional tissues from serial slices, and infer spatial transcriptomics directly from histology images.

01

Gene expression enhancement

stPainter imputes unmeasured genes and denoises spatial transcriptomics at single-cell resolution.

02

3D spatial reconstruction

DeepSpatial transforms discrete serial sections into continuous three-dimensional tissue atlases.

03

Histology-to-ST generation

DriftST predicts spatial gene-expression distributions from H&E pathology images in one step.
