What we study

We work across single-cell and spatial omics to overcome sparse measurement, missing tissue depth, and experimental cost. Our goal is to build reusable machine-learning methods that preserve biological context and turn complex data into testable discovery.

Biological question Omics measurement Computational modeling Biological insight
01

Enhance what can be measured

stPainter transfers knowledge from a pan-cancer single-cell atlas to recover unmeasured genes, denoise sparse observations, and reveal fine-grained cell states.

02

Reconstruct what sectioning removes

DeepSpatial models tissue as a continuous process, converting serial two-dimensional sections into a true three-dimensional cellular and molecular atlas.

03

Infer omics from routine histology

DriftST learns gene dependencies and predicts spatial expression from H&E images in one generative step, at both spot and single-cell resolution.

04

Build general intelligent-omics models

We explore multimodal representation learning, biological foundation models, and robust benchmarks that connect molecular measurements with tissue phenotype.
