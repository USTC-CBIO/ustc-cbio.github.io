Our mission

Biology is becoming data-rich. Understanding it is the next frontier.

IBO Lab sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, computational biology, and omics. We design models that connect molecular measurements across scales—from cells and tissues to patients and populations.

What we study

From data to discovery.

We develop computational approaches that make high-dimensional biology more understandable, predictive, and actionable.

Single-cell intelligence

Learning cellular states, trajectories, and interactions from single-cell and spatial omics.
