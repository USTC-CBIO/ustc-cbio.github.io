IBO LAB · RESEARCH

Intelligence that makes biology understandable.

We build computational methods to reveal structure, mechanism, and meaning in high-dimensional biological data.

Our approach

Biological systems operate across many scales and modalities. We combine machine learning with biological knowledge to connect sequence, expression, spatial organization, imaging, and clinical context—while keeping the resulting models interpretable and scientifically useful.

01

Single-cell intelligence

Learning cell states, transitions, and interactions from single-cell RNA, epigenomic, and spatial measurements.

02

Multimodal foundation models

Building transferable representations that integrate biological sequences, molecular profiles, and images.

03

Interpretable discovery

Turning predictive signals into mechanisms, biomarkers, and testable hypotheses for experiments.
